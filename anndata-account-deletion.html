My Anndata – Account Deletion Request

To request the deletion of your My Anndata account and all associated data, please email us at awarediainnovations@gmail.com.

Steps to request deletion

  1. Send an email to awarediainnovations@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account.
  2. Use the subject line "Account Deletion Request".
  3. Please include your username.

Data Retention Policy

Upon receiving your request, we will delete your account credentials, profile information, and saved preferences, health information, and product scan history from our database within 30 days. No data will be retained after the deletion process is complete.
